Additional Pelliron programmes and account options

Additional options are available for different trading formats and levels of user capital.

Loss-compensation option for qualifying accounts

Once the programme conditions are met, a user may receive compensation for loss-making trades.

1+1 programme

Extra funds are released progressively as the stated turnover conditions are met.

VIP Club

Club participants receive dedicated terms, percentage-based accruals and reimbursement of part of their transaction costs.

Account Priority

This status is intended for accounts funded with at least USD 10,000 and covers a separate cash-back programme.

Double Hit

The programme adds funds to the account and expands the trading capital available to the user.
