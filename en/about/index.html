Background and structure of Pelliron Universal LLC

Pelliron Universal LLC presents itself as an international brokerage business supported by specialists with experience in trading, investment and client execution services.

Stages in the company’s development

Pelliron Universal LLC was formed after a number of analytical and investment projects. Its founders combined experience from several trading and brokerage fields to build a service around practical user requirements.

Approach to user service

The service is designed around routine trader requirements, with emphasis on clear navigation, operational security, timely order handling and direct communication with the team.

Markets and financial instruments

Pelliron provides access to currency pairs, exchange-traded assets and commodity instruments. The service also covers options for investment planning, hedging and private capital management.

Access across multiple devices

Pelliron Trading Platform can be used through a web browser, on phones running iOS or Android, devices using Windows or macOS, and tablets.

Order processing and user assistance

The company prioritises order execution quality, protection of user funds and assistance at any time of day. Its team assists with technical tasks and administrative enquiries.

Objectives of the Pelliron service

The company aims to provide a practical working environment for international market trading and bring the necessary tools together within one service.
