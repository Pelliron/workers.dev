Rules for cookies and localStorage

Revision date: 22 July 2026. The website uses only the technical mechanisms needed to retain the visitor’s selected preference.

1. How cookie files work2. Technical mechanisms in use on this website3. Website language settings4. External websites and services5. Managing your saved choice6. Changes to this document

1. How cookie files work

Cookies are small files that a website may store through a web browser. localStorage performs a similar function by keeping information locally on the visitor’s device.

2. Technical mechanisms in use on this website

The static build records the chosen notice option in localStorage under pellironCookieChoice. This prevents repeated banner displays, while advertising, behavioural and analytics cookies remain absent from the code.

3. Website language settings

The active path — /, /uk/, /ro/ or /en/ — determines the displayed language. Changing the language does not create an additional visitor identifier.

4. External websites and services

Following an outbound link or starting an email client may open a third-party service governed by its own cookie rules. Those settings are outside this website’s control.

5. Managing your saved choice

The notice provides controls for allowing or rejecting technical storage. Removing the localStorage entry in browser settings causes the banner to be shown again.

6. Changes to this document

Any future use of analytics, embedded media, maps or advertising requires a prior revision of this policy and the consent process.
