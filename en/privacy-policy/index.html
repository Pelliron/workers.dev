Personal information processing rules

Revision date: 22 July 2026. This document explains what information may be processed when visitors use the Pelliron website and its communication channels.

1. Area of application of this policy2. Data that may be logged3. Reasons for using the information4. Legal basis5. Retention periods and safeguards6. External service providers7. Visitor rights and choices8. Third-party resources and policy changes

1. Area of application of this policy

This document covers information generated while browsing the Pelliron website or using the communication channels displayed on its pages.

2. Data that may be logged

Hosting logs may contain the IP address, browser type, request time, requested URL and server response. Contact-form fields are used only to prepare a local email draft and are not transmitted to the website server.

3. Reasons for using the information

Operational data supports security, fault diagnosis and reliable page delivery. It is not used as an input for automated financial decisions.

4. Legal basis

Limited server logging is processed under the legitimate interest of protecting and maintaining the website. Email communication begins only when the visitor chooses to send a message.

5. Retention periods and safeguards

Log-retention periods are set by the hosting configuration. Organisational and technical safeguards reduce exposure, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely risk-free.

6. External service providers

Processing may involve the hosting company and the email provider selected by the visitor. Personal data is neither sold nor automatically forwarded to the broker.

7. Visitor rights and choices

Where applicable law permits, visitors may request access, amendment, erasure or restricted processing and may submit an objection. Such requests can be sent to the email listed in the contact section.

8. Third-party resources and policy changes

External links open resources governed by separate privacy terms. This policy may be amended when website functionality or legal obligations are updated.
